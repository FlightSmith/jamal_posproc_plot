Spanwise loads and chordwise Cp

Cp = (local pressure − infout p) / qdin. Pressure forces from every section element are summed and projected to lift at ALPHA; cl = lift / (qdin × chord). The section chord is the overall Xmax − Xmin across all elements. Viscous shear is excluded.

Pressure and section files must use matching point order. Loads require X aft, section ordinate upward, and BETA = 0. Local/canted section coordinates need an explicit body-axis mapping.

Add the POLARs/components to compare as overlays, then choose a common ALPHA or CL. Each curve uses its own sweep; values outside its range or with ambiguous CL crossings are unavailable.

Confirm the leading-edge direction for your geometry. Changing this control only changes the Cp display; surface point order is preserved. All elements share the same section x/c, so their gaps and overlap remain visible. All components use infout BREF for span normalization. Cp station labels follow the selected span coordinate.
